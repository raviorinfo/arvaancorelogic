1. Introduction & Scope

Arvaan Core Logic ("we", "our", or "us") operates this website (the "Site") and is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of information we receive from users of the Site.

By accessing and using Arvaan Core Logic, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please refrain from using our services.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you contact us, request a technical consultation, subscribe to our technical newsletter, or fill out a form, you may be asked to provide personal identifiers including:

  • Full name and professional job title
  • Company or organization name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Project specifications, architectural requirements, and budget ranges

B. Log Files & Technical Usage Data

Like standard web publishers, Arvaan Core Logic maintains log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, operating system, and the number of clicks. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information and is utilized strictly for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking user movement, and gathering demographic metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Providing, operating, maintaining, and improving our website and services
  • Responding to your inquiries and scheduling technical consultations
  • Sending you engineering updates, technical articles, and newsletter digests (with your consent)
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing technical issues, fraud, and malicious activity
  • Complying with statutory and legal obligations

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Google AdSense

Arvaan Core Logic uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, record user-specific information on which pages the visitor accesses or visits, and customize web page content based on browser type or other technical indicators.

Mandatory Google AdSense & Advertising Disclosure:

We work with third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements on our website. Please review the following policies regarding Google's use of advertising cookies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables Google and its partner network to serve personalized ads to our visitors based on their browsing behavior on Arvaan Core Logic and other web properties.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, visitors can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance consumer choice tool at aboutads.info/choices or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at networkadvertising.org/choices.

For more detailed information regarding cookie management, please refer to our dedicated Cookie Policy.

6. GDPR Compliance & Data Protection Rights (EEA & UK)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK), you possess certain statutory data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Arvaan Core Logic takes reasonable measures to allow you to correct, amend, delete, or limit the use of your Personal Data:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure ("Right to be forgotten"): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.
  • The right to object: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. Please contact our privacy team at privacy@arvaancorelogic.com to exercise these rights.

7. CCPA & CPRA California Consumer Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are granted specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about you in the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You may request the deletion of your personal data collected, subject to statutory exemptions.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you in pricing, service quality, or availability for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: Arvaan Core Logic does not sell your personal information. We do not sell consumer personal data to third parties for financial consideration.

8. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, strict access control, and periodic vulnerability audits. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, after which data is securely purged or anonymized.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arvaan Core Logic does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that Arvaan Core Logic has in its database the personal identification of a child under the age of 13, please contact us immediately and we will use our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
